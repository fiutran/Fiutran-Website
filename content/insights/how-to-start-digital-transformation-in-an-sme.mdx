## Why do most SME transformations stall? <Draft />

They start with tools instead of the business. A new system is bought, nobody changes how they work, and the project quietly ends. The fix is to start with how work actually happens today.

<Ph label="SME transformation statistic with source" />

## Step 1: How do you map how the business really runs? <Draft />

Spend time with the people who do the work. Map the five to ten processes that drive revenue and cost, and note where time, money or customers are lost.

## Step 2: How do you pick the first win? <Draft />

Choose one process where the pain is visible, the data mostly exists and success can be measured within a quarter. Avoid the biggest problem first; prove the approach on a winnable one.

## Step 3: How do you build it without a big IT team? <Draft />

Build the smallest working version, put it in front of real users within weeks, and improve it. A partner or [CTO on demand](/cto-on-demand/) can provide the leadership and team you do not have in-house.

## Step 4: How do you scale what works? <Draft />

Once the first win is running, use the same method on the next process, and invest in [data foundations](/services/data-foundations/) so each step gets easier.

## What should the first 90 days look like? <Draft />

1. Weeks 1–3: understand and map the business
2. Weeks 4–5: pick and design the first win
3. Weeks 6–11: build and launch it
4. Weeks 12–13: measure, decide the next step
