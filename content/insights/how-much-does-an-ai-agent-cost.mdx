## What drives the cost of an AI agent? <Draft />

Five things drive almost all of the cost:

1. **Scope**: how many tasks the agent handles and how complex they are
2. **Integrations**: how many systems it must read from or write to
3. **Guardrails**: the checks, approvals and human hand-off it needs
4. **Channels**: WhatsApp, web, email or internal tools
5. **Usage**: conversation volume, which drives model and messaging costs

## How do you budget a first pilot? <Draft />

Pick one task with measurable value, such as replying to enquiries within a minute. Budget for the build, three months of running, and a review. If it pays back, expand scope. See [AI agents and automation](/services/ai-agents-and-automation/) and [engagement models](/engagement-models/).

## Is it cheaper to buy an off-the-shelf chatbot? <Draft />

Sometimes. If a tool already fits your process and data, buy it. If it cannot see your products, prices or stock, a custom agent usually costs less over time. See [build vs buy AI](/insights/build-vs-buy-ai/).
