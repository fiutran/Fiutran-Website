## What does a CTO on demand actually do? <Draft />

A CTO on demand sets technical direction, makes build-versus-buy and vendor decisions, manages technical risk and security, and leads delivery. The difference from a full-time hire is the model: you get senior leadership for an agreed share of time, and with Fiutran, a team that builds and runs what is decided.

- Technology strategy and roadmap tied to business goals
- Architecture, vendor and tool decisions
- Hiring, coordinating or replacing external vendors
- Security, data protection and technical risk
- Hands-on delivery through the Fiutran team

## How is it different from a fractional CTO? <Draft />

A fractional CTO is usually one experienced person working part-time. A CTO on demand, as Fiutran runs it, combines that leadership with designers, engineers and data people who ship. You are not left with a plan and no one to execute it.

## When does a business need one? <Draft />

Typical signals: you are making expensive technology decisions without a technical leader, vendors disagree and nobody can referee, a product or platform is stuck, or AI is on the agenda and nobody owns it.
