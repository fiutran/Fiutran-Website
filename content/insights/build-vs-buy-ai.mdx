## When should you buy an AI tool? <Draft />

Buy when the tool already fits how you work, the data it needs is generic or easy to connect, and the task is common across businesses, such as transcription, writing help or generic support.

## When should you build? <Draft />

Build when the AI needs your own data, systems and rules to be useful, when the process is your competitive advantage, or when off-the-shelf tools would force you to change how you work.

## Can you combine both? <Draft />

Usually that is the best answer: buy the models and platforms, build the thin layer that connects them to your data, processes and guardrails.

## How do you decide? A decision table <Draft />

| Question | If yes, lean towards |
|---|---|
| Does a tool already fit the process with little change? | Buy |
| Does it need your private data or systems? | Build (on bought models) |
| Is the process a competitive advantage? | Build |
| Do you need it live this month with no integration? | Buy |
| Will per-seat costs grow faster than value? | Build |

## What are the hidden costs on each side? <Draft />

Buying hides costs in seats, workarounds and lock-in. Building hides costs in maintenance and ownership, which is why Fiutran builds and also runs what it builds.
