## What is SEO? <Draft />

Search engine optimisation makes pages easy for search engines to crawl, understand and rank, so they appear in the list of results people click.

## What is GEO? <Draft />

Generative engine optimisation makes content easy for AI systems, such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, to find, trust and cite inside the answer itself.

## How do GEO and SEO differ in practice? <Draft />

| | SEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Rank in a list of links | Be cited inside an AI answer |
| Unit | The page | The passage, fact or table |
| Signals | Links, relevance, experience | Clarity, consistency, structure, authority |
| Format | Keyword-led pages | Question-led pages with direct answers |
| Measurement | Rankings, clicks | Citations, mentions, referral traffic |

## What about AEO and GSEO? <Draft />

Answer engine optimisation (AEO) targets featured snippets and voice answers with short, direct answers. GSEO, as Fiutran uses the term, targets Google’s generative surfaces (AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini) with structured data, freshness and E-E-A-T signals. See the [glossary](/insights/glossary/).

## What should a business do about both? <Draft />

- Answer one real question per page, directly, near the top
- Keep company facts identical everywhere (see [Fiutran at a glance](/fiutran-at-a-glance/))
- Use structured data and plain, crawlable HTML
- Allow AI crawlers in robots.txt and publish an llms.txt
- Publish comparisons, prices and sourced statistics people want to quote
